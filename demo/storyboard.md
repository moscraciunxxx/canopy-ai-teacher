# Canopy two-minute demo storyboard

The spoken line for every beat is stored in `vo_script.txt`. Final shot length
follows the synthesized speech; no line is padded or time-stretched.

| Beat | Picture and interaction | Must-hear / must-see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Promise | Public Canopy URL, full hero and academy/course rail. Scroll into the learning lab. | Canopy, living model, evidence trail |
| 02 · Language | Open the language selector, choose Romanian, then Arabic. Hold on the fully localized physics lab and RTL shell. | twenty languages; control, chart label, feedback message, source |
| 03 · Physics | Return to English. Move launch speed and angle; rotate the 3D trajectory until the barrier and landing zone are clear. | trajectory, barrier clearance, target landing, teacher insight |
| 04 · Biology | Switch to Biology. Rotate the helix, move the selected base-pair position, then choose Variant. | DNA helix, base pair, original, variant |
| 05 · Humanities | Switch to Human Worlds, History. Toggle from Include disagreement to Only supportive. Then switch to Civics. | source constellation, perspective, repeated posts, independent evidence |
| 06 · Ethics + Coach | Open Ethics, change rights and uncertainty, then scroll to Coach and reveal the prediction/evidence/model scaffold. | outcomes, rights, uncertainty, prediction, evidence |
| 07 · Architecture | Show the six-node learning constellation, mastery signal, and teacher brief/source shelf. | course-specific reasoning graph, mastery, next useful move |
| 08 · Transfer | Sweep through Learn, Remix, and Apply/Roleplay; finish on the Canopy hero with nine courses and twenty languages visible. | AI tutor you can inspect, nine subjects, twenty languages, ordinary browser |

## Capture rules

- Capture the public judge URL, never localhost.
- Keep the browser at a clean desktop viewport with no personal tabs visible.
- Use actual app interactions; do not fake values in post-production.
- Add captions from the exact synthesized line and Whisper-check each line.
- Final runtime must be below 120 seconds.
