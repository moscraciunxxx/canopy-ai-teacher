## Title

Canopy — Private AI Teacher Across 9 Subjects & 20 Languages | Demo

## Description

Canopy is a private, multilingual AI teacher that turns nine school subjects
into interactive, inspectable reasoning journeys—without accounts or API keys.

In this 1:48 walkthrough:

- follow six-stage learning constellations through validation and transfer;
- switch the full learning surface across 20 languages, including Romanian and
  right-to-left Arabic;
- inspect evidence, confidence, misconception hypotheses, and mastery;
- run optional in-browser AI through Transformers.js, ONNX, WebGPU, or WASM;
- turn teacher material into six editable Course Forge stages;
- inspect honest observed metrics, a device-local learner model, exports, and
  accessibility tools.
